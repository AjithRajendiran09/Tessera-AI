About the Project

Tessera AI is an AI-powered research paper management and intelligence platform built for PhD scholars, researchers, and academics. It transforms the traditionally manual process of literature review into an intelligent, data-driven workflow — helping you organize, analyze, and extract insights from your entire research paper collection across any domain.

The name Tessera comes from the Latin word for a small tile used to create mosaics. Just as individual tiles come together to form a complete picture, each research paper you add becomes a piece of a larger intellectual mosaic — revealing patterns, gaps, and connections across your field of study.

Key Capabilities

🤖

AI-Powered Parsing

Upload any PDF and Google Gemini AI extracts title, authors, venue, DOI, URL, contribution, limitations, and auto-assigns the research domain.

📊

Visual Analytics

Interactive dashboard with domain distribution charts, publication timelines, relevance scoring, and read-progress tracking.

🗂️

Smart Domains

Automatically creates new research domains when papers don't fit existing categories. Export domain-specific papers to Excel.

🔬

Gap Tracking

Systematically track research gaps with severity levels and resolution status to guide your research contributions.
